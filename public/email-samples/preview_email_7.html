However you voted, thank you. Here's what happens as results are made official.

Thank You

Thank you for showing up, Detroit.

However you voted this election, early, by mail, or on Election Day, thank you for being part of it. Every ballot is a Detroiter making their voice heard.

Democracy doesn't end when the polls close. Staying informed and engaged is how we keep building the city we want to see, together.

 

When results become official

Results aren't final on election night. Votes are reviewed and certified by the Board of Canvassers in the days after the election, a careful process that makes sure every valid vote is counted.
