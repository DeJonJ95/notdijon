Early voting sites are open July 25 through August 2. Here's your quick checklist.

Early Voting

Early voting starts tomorrow.

Detroit's early voting sites open Friday, July 25, and stay open through Saturday, August 2. You don't have to wait for Election Day. Vote in person, on your schedule, at any early voting site in the city.

 

Your early-voting checklist

✓Bring a photo ID if you have one. Don't have one? You can still vote by signing a short affidavit.
✓Know what's on your ballot: review your sample ballot before you go.
✓Confirm your voter registration is current and lists your correct address.
✓Give yourself a few minutes. Early voting sites are open to any registered Detroit voter.

Any registered Detroit voter can use any early voting site in the city. Find the location and hours that work best for you.
