Plan Your Vote

Key dates you need to know for August 4.

Election Day is Tuesday, August 4, but in Michigan you have more than one way to make your voice heard. Here's how to plan ahead and choose what works for you.

 

Three ways to vote in Detroit

1
 Vote early, in person
Detroit's early voting sites are open July 25 through August 2. Walk in, cast your ballot, and you're done. No need to wait for Election Day.
2
 Vote absentee, by mail
Every registered Michigan voter can vote absentee. Request your ballot, fill it out at home, and return it by mail or to a secure drop box.
3
 Vote on Election Day
Polls are open 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. on Tuesday, August 4. Head to your assigned precinct and cast your ballot in person.
