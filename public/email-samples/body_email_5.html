Final Days

Only three days left to vote early.

Early voting in Detroit ends Saturday, August 2. If voting before Election Day fits your schedule, now is the time. After that, your next chance is Election Day: Tuesday, August 4, with polls open 8 a.m. to 8 p.m.

 

Get the facts

“You can't vote without a photo ID.”

In Michigan, you can vote without a photo ID by signing a brief affidavit at the polls. Your ballot counts the same as everyone else's.
