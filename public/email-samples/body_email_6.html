Election Day

Tomorrow is Election Day.

Tuesday, August 4 is Election Day in Detroit, with polls open from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. If you haven't voted yet, tomorrow is your day. Here's what to expect when you arrive.

 

What to expect at the polls

•Bring a photo ID if you have one. If you don't, you can sign an affidavit and still vote.
•If you're in line by 8 p.m., you have the right to vote — stay in line.
•Need help? Poll workers are there to assist you — ask questions any time.
•Have your sample ballot or notes ready so you can move through quickly.

Polls are open 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. Vote at your assigned precinct — look up your location before you head out.
